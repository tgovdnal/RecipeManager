Creamy Asparagus Risotto

A celebration of spring on a plate. Velvety Arborio rice folded with vibrant asparagus purée, finished with aged Pecorino and a hint of lemon.

Prep Time

15 min

Cook Time

30 min

Servings

4 People

Ingredients

  • check
    1.5 cups Arborio rice
  • check
    1 lb fresh Asparagus, trimmed
  • check
    5-6 cups Vegetable broth, warm
  • check
    1/2 cup Dry white wine (Pinot Grigio)
  • check
    2 Shallots, finely minced
  • check
    1/2 cup Grated Pecorino Romano
  • check
    2 tbsp Unsalted butter

Nutritional Info (per serving)

Calories 380 kcal
Proteins 12g
Fats 14g
Carbohydrates 52g

The Preparation

01

Prepare the Asparagus

Blanch the asparagus spears in boiling salted water for 2 minutes. Reserve the tips for garnish and blend the stalks with a splash of broth until smooth and vibrant green.

02

The Soffritto

Sauté finely minced shallots in butter until translucent. Add the Arborio rice and toast for 2-3 minutes until the edges are transparent, releasing a nutty aroma.

03

The Slow Pour

Deglaze with white wine. Once absorbed, add warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring constantly. This friction is what creates the legendary creaminess of a true risotto.

04

The Finish (Mantecatura)

Stir in the asparagus purée for that brilliant green hue. Remove from heat and beat in cold butter and Pecorino Romano vigorously. Fold in the reserved asparagus tips and serve immediately.
